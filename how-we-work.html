How we work

You approve each step. You keep what each one makes. You decide whether there is a next one.

The rule

No step depends on the one after it.

Every engagement is sold one step at a time. Each step has a fixed price, agreed before it starts. Each ends with something that is yours whether or not you go on, and with a decision about the next step, ours as much as yours. There is no project that turns into a second job for the person who runs your business.

A fixed price, before we start

Each step is quoted on its own. The build is priced from what discovery counted, so nobody is guessing at the size of your data.

Something you keep, every time

A map, a count, a working piece of the fix, the reporting itself. It stays with you if you stop after any step.

A real choice at every gate

You can stop. We can stop. Nobody owes the next step to the last one, and the gate is where we say so.

The three steps

Three steps. Each one fixed price, and each one ends with something you keep.

You approve each step before it starts. Each ends with deliverables that are yours, and with a decision, ours as much as yours, about whether to go on.

1

Discovery

  • A map of what each system holds and how they connect.
  • A number for how much of your business your systems can measure today, how much they can't, and why.
  • A gap list: everything that doesn't match or can't be priced, with the volume behind each.

2

Test, learn and plan

  • One piece of the fix done by hand, walked through with the person who would use it.
  • A fix plan: what closes each gap, in what order, and where it helps.
  • A fixed price for the build, quantified rather than estimated.

3

Build

  • The reporting itself, on a schedule.
  • The weekly list each manager works from.
  • The month-end check, systems side by side, before the books close.
  • Training for your own person until they can run a cycle without us in the room.

Most data work is quoted before anyone has seen the data. Here, discovery is the quote. By the end of it we know every system you run, every gap between them, and how big each one is, so the build is priced on what is actually there. You keep that picture whether or not you continue.

What keeps it fixed

Keeping it fixed is a step too.

After the build you can take what you keep and stop. Or we keep your systems connected and your numbers current, for a monthly fee quoted from how many systems you run and how often you need them refreshed. It runs month to month: the same choice you had at every gate, made every month. It is a separate line from the work, on every proposal and every invoice, so the work never has to be done again to keep the numbers current.

If you have your own analyst, or a firm you already trust, that monthly service is available on its own. The first step still happens, because somebody has to connect the systems. See how we work with firms.

If it ever ends

You leave with your data and its full history, the reports built on it, and every definition in a form you can read and reuse. What stays with us is the machinery and the running of it, which is the service you stop paying for.

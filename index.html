For operators

Managed analytics, handled.

Most operations run on five or ten systems that were never built to talk to each other. Getting a straight answer means somebody exports it all and reconciles it by hand, then does it again next month. We take that over.

One customer, one record
Your reservation system, your CRM, and your POS each spell the same person differently. We resolve them into a single governed record per person; every merge is reviewed by a human and reversible. The industry calls this master data management; you get it without running an MDM program.
We define it with you
A working session to pin down the questions that matter and how each number is calculated. Your definitions, written down and enforced.
Reporting you actually use
We build the dashboards and reports that answer your questions, and we keep them working as the business changes. What we commit to is the work, not a fixed list of outputs, because nobody can name the right reports before seeing the data.
It stays current
Refreshed on your schedule. When a definition needs to change because the business changed, we change it; that is part of keeping it current, not a new project.
Beyond that scope
Deeper analysis is quoted separately, or connect your own tools directly; Power BI, Tableau, Excel, your accountant.

Who this is for: operators big enough to have real questions and too small to justify a data team. Multi-location restaurants and hospitality groups, resorts and destination organizations.

How you can check the numbers

Whichever door you came through, the numbers work the same way.

A person reviews it
Every decision about how your systems fit together is approved by a human before it reaches you, and you see the reasoning.
Any number is traceable
Back to the system it came from, and back to the decision that produced it.
Systems that disagree
When two of your systems report the same thing differently, we show you the gap rather than quietly picking one.
Wrong calls reverse
Every decision is stored. If one turns out to be wrong, it rolls back without rebuilding anything.

We will never tell you a number is right; a number is only as accurate as the system it came from, and those systems are yours. What we can show you, on demand, is exactly where it came from and exactly what was applied to it.

What it costs

Two numbers: the platform, and the reporting on top.

The platform is $3,000 per month, sized to the number of systems you run. That covers bringing your systems together, resolving identity, enforcing your definitions, and keeping every number traceable to its source.

The reporting built and maintained on top is quoted separately, because what one operation needs is nothing like the next. Standing it up is a one-time fee that depends on the state of your systems; we price it once we have seen them, and you see the number before anything starts.

No long-term contract required, and the monthly number does not move unless your business does.

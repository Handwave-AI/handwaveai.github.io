How you can check the numbers

The next time someone questions a number, the answer is already in the report.

Every number comes with its receipt: which system it came from, what was done to it, and who signed off.

A person reviews it
Every decision about how your systems fit together is approved by a human before it reaches you, and you see the reasoning.
Any number can be walked back
Back to the system it came from, and back to the decision that produced it.
Systems that disagree
When two of your systems report the same thing differently, we show you the gap instead of quietly picking one.
Wrong calls reverse
Every decision is stored. If one turns out to be wrong, it rolls back without rebuilding anything.

We will never tell you a number is right. We will show you where it came from and what was done to it.
